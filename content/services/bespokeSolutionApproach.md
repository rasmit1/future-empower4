# The Bespoke Solution Approach

Every embedded engagement follows the similar underlying approach, even though the specific content is different every time — that's exactly what makes it bespoke, not templated.

![The Bespoke Solution Approach](/images/bespoke_solution_approachonly.png)

**Challenges and Impacts & Symptoms** — the real, often entangled situation an organization is actually in, separated into root causes and the effects those causes are producing. These aren't the same thing, and treating them as one blurs the diagnosis.

**Embedded Partner** — not a consultant who hands over a deck and leaves. The role is to diagnose the real problem underneath the stated mandate, align leadership — including control partners — on shared goals, and own delivery through completion, not a handoff.

**Approach** — the specific method applied to move from diagnosis to result. This is where the real work happens, and it looks different in every engagement because every organization's constraints are different.

**Outcomes** — what actually changes as a result, flowing out from the work the partner and the approach produced.
